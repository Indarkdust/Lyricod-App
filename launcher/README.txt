Run Lyricod.exe and keep the app folder beside it.
You can update Lyricod from Settings > About.
Portable copy: you can also close the app and replace the whole Lyricod folder. An installer is available if you prefer it.
Installed copy: to remove Lyricod, use Windows Settings > Apps.

运行 Lyricod.exe，并保留旁边的 app 文件夹。
可以在「设置 > 关于」中更新。
便携版：也可以退出程序后替换整个 Lyricod 文件夹。如果不想使用便携版，也可以下载安装版。
安装版：如需卸载，请前往 Windows「设置 > 应用」。
