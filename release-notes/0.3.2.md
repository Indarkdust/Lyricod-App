Videos aren't treated as songs any more.

- **Videos stay videos.** Windows reports a video playing in your browser the same way as a song, so Lyricod used to search lyrics for it, show "Searching for lyrics…" and then the video's title on the island and the desktop strip, and sometimes even another song's lyrics. Now, for anything that isn't a music player, Lyricod only shows lyrics when it finds a confident match: the title, plus the artist or the length. Otherwise it treats what's playing as not a song, and the island and desktop lyrics stay out of the way. The tray panel and lyrics window still show what's playing, and you can still choose lyrics for it yourself.
- **Music videos still work.** An official music video, or a song in a web player, gets its lyrics as before when the title and artist match ("Rick Astley - Never Gonna Give You Up (Official Video)" finds the song's lyrics and follows the video).
- Music players (NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, Spotify, Apple Music and others) work exactly as before.
- Settings › About › Diagnostics now says whether the player counts as a music player, whether what's playing is treated as a song, and how well the best lyrics matched.

### Download

- **Lyricod-0.3.2-windows-x64-setup.exe**: the installer, for most Windows PCs (Intel and AMD).
- **Lyricod-0.3.2-windows-arm64-setup.exe**: the installer, for Arm PCs (Snapdragon, Copilot+ PCs).
- **Lyricod-0.3.2-windows-x64.zip** and **Lyricod-0.3.2-windows-arm64.zip**: the same, without installing. Unzip it anywhere and run `Lyricod.exe` in the Lyricod folder, keeping the `app` folder next to it.

Lyricod lives in the notification area. Your settings and saved lyrics are kept whichever you choose. From 0.3.1 on, Lyricod updates itself from Settings › About or the tray panel; to update from 0.3.0 or earlier, quit Lyricod, then replace its whole folder with the new one, or run the installer. If you move from the zip to the installer and had Open at login on, turn it off and on again in Settings › General. It needs Windows 10 (version 1809) or later; the island's bars follow the music on Windows 11. Lyricod isn't signed yet, so Windows may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.
