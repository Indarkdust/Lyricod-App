Choose the lyrics font, move around a song from the progress bar, and a neater island.

- **Lyrics font.** Lyrics now use Microsoft YaHei by default (Yu Gothic UI for Japanese lines, Malgun Gothic for Korean ones), and Settings › Lyrics › Lyrics font lets you pick any font on your PC. MiSans by Xiaomi, which earlier versions came with, is the recommended choice there: choosing it offers to download it from Xiaomi (about 15 MB, under Xiaomi's licence), and Remove MiSans deletes it again. If you liked how lyrics looked before, choose MiSans.
- **Move around a song from the progress bar.** Drag or click the progress bar in the lyrics window, the island's mini player or the tray panel, and the player jumps there, just as clicking a lyric does. Players that can't be moved from outside (QQ Music, for one) say so when you point at the bar.
- **The island's cover lines up with its corner.** The cover sits as far from the island's side as from its bottom, and its rounded corner follows the island's, as on the Mac.
- **Licence.** Lyricod for Windows is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. The downloads include the licence and the notices for the parts it uses, in the `app` folder.

### Download

- **Lyricod-0.3.0-windows-x64-setup.exe**: the installer, for most Windows PCs (Intel and AMD).
- **Lyricod-0.3.0-windows-arm64-setup.exe**: the installer, for Arm PCs (Snapdragon, Copilot+ PCs).
- **Lyricod-0.3.0-windows-x64.zip** and **Lyricod-0.3.0-windows-arm64.zip**: the same, without installing. Unzip it anywhere and run `Lyricod.exe` in the Lyricod folder, keeping the `app` folder next to it.

Lyricod lives in the notification area. Your settings and saved lyrics are kept whichever you choose. To update the zip version, quit Lyricod, then replace its whole folder with the new one. If you move from the zip to the installer and had Open at login on, turn it off and on again in Settings › General. It needs Windows 10 (version 1809) or later; the island's bars follow the music on Windows 11. Lyricod isn't signed yet, so Windows may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.
