Fixes since 0.2.0.

- Lyrics drifted out of step with NetEase Cloud Music on some PCs. When NetEase is set to tell Windows its playback position, it can show up twice in Windows' media controls, once with the position and once without, and Lyricod could follow the one without and time the song itself. It now follows the one with the position, so the lyrics keep up, including when you skip around in a song.
- Covers are sharp again with NetEase: Lyricod takes the cover from that same session (with a progress plugin like InfLink-rs, that's the full-resolution one) rather than a small thumbnail.
- The lyrics window in full screen (or any wide window) is balanced like the Mac's: the cover and the lyrics sit together in the middle, and the cover, title and controls grow with the screen.
- Settings › About › Diagnostics now says whether the player tells Lyricod its position, and how big the cover is.

### Download

- **Lyricod-0.2.1-windows-x64-setup.exe**: the installer, for most Windows PCs (Intel and AMD).
- **Lyricod-0.2.1-windows-arm64-setup.exe**: the installer, for Arm PCs (Snapdragon, Copilot+ PCs).
- **Lyricod-0.2.1-windows-x64.zip** and **Lyricod-0.2.1-windows-arm64.zip**: the same, without installing. Unzip it anywhere and run `Lyricod.exe` in the Lyricod folder, keeping the `app` folder next to it.

Lyricod lives in the notification area. Your settings and saved lyrics are kept whichever you choose. To update the zip version, quit Lyricod, then replace its whole folder with the new one. If you move from the zip to the installer and had Open at login on, turn it off and on again in Settings › General. It needs Windows 10 (version 1809) or later; the island's bars follow the music on Windows 11. Lyricod isn't signed yet, so Windows may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Lyrics use the MiSans font by Xiaomi (its licence is in `app/Assets/Fonts`).
