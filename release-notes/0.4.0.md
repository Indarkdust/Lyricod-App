Hover by direction, your own island colour, and safer updates.

- **Hover by direction.** A new choice in Settings › Lyricod Island › When hovering: coming onto the island from below hides it, so you can use what's underneath, and coming from the left or right expands it into the mini player.
- **No second cover in the mini player.** When the island expands, the small cover and the bars at the top fade away, so the cover only shows once.
- **Your own island colour.** Turn off "Colour lyrics from the album cover" in Settings › Lyricod Island and pick a colour for the island's lyrics and bars, separate from the desktop lyrics. If you'd turned it off before, the island keeps the colour it had.
- **Your choice about other players.** A new setting in Settings › Lyrics, "Look up lyrics for browsers and other apps" (on by default): when something plays outside a music player, Lyricod sends its title and artist or channel to NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music and LRCLIB to see whether it's a song. Turn it off to look up lyrics only for music players.
- **NetEase Cloud Music.** When NetEase isn't sharing its position, so the lyrics can drift, the tray panel links to a short guide on fixing that with the InfLink-rs plugin.
- **Safer updates.** When the zip version updates itself, the old version is kept until the new one has started properly; if it doesn't, the old one is put back and started.
- **Fixes.** Lyrics you picked and timing you adjusted for a song are kept when the player corrects the song's length a moment later. Lyrics with broken timestamps no longer upset the display, and unusually large lyric downloads are refused.
- **Licences.** The downloads include the full licence texts and notices of everything they contain, in the `app\licenses` folder.

### Download

- **Lyricod-0.4.0-windows-x64-setup.exe**: the installer, for most Windows PCs (Intel and AMD).
- **Lyricod-0.4.0-windows-arm64-setup.exe**: the installer, for Arm PCs (Snapdragon, Copilot+ PCs).
- **Lyricod-0.4.0-windows-x64.zip** and **Lyricod-0.4.0-windows-arm64.zip**: the same, without installing. Unzip it anywhere and run `Lyricod.exe` in the Lyricod folder, keeping the `app` folder next to it.

Lyricod lives in the notification area. Your settings and saved lyrics are kept whichever you choose. From 0.3.1 on, Lyricod updates itself from Settings › About or the tray panel; to update from 0.3.0 or earlier, quit Lyricod, then replace its whole folder with the new one, or run the installer. If you move from the zip to the installer and had Open at login on, turn it off and on again in Settings › General. It needs Windows 10 (version 1809) or later; the island's bars follow the music on Windows 11. Lyricod isn't signed yet, so Windows may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.
