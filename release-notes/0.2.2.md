Changes since 0.2.1.

- The island goes away when the music stops: a moment after you pause, it fades out with a slight shrink toward the top of the screen, and comes back as soon as the music plays again. The short wait keeps it from blinking between songs, and it stays while the pointer is on it. You can turn this off in Settings › Lyricod Island › Hide when paused.
- With "Bars follow the music" on, the bars lie still between songs instead of bouncing on their own while there's nothing to hear. The bounce is now only for PCs where Lyricod can't listen to the player.

### Download

- **Lyricod-0.2.2-windows-x64-setup.exe**: the installer, for most Windows PCs (Intel and AMD).
- **Lyricod-0.2.2-windows-arm64-setup.exe**: the installer, for Arm PCs (Snapdragon, Copilot+ PCs).
- **Lyricod-0.2.2-windows-x64.zip** and **Lyricod-0.2.2-windows-arm64.zip**: the same, without installing. Unzip it anywhere and run `Lyricod.exe` in the Lyricod folder, keeping the `app` folder next to it.

Lyricod lives in the notification area. Your settings and saved lyrics are kept whichever you choose. To update the zip version, quit Lyricod, then replace its whole folder with the new one. If you move from the zip to the installer and had Open at login on, turn it off and on again in Settings › General. It needs Windows 10 (version 1809) or later; the island's bars follow the music on Windows 11. Lyricod isn't signed yet, so Windows may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Lyrics use the MiSans font by Xiaomi (its licence is in `app/Assets/Fonts`).
